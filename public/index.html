Saborosa

Top-down action puzzler · PC · Solo & couch co-op planned

A 2D top-down game with isometric-style visuals, built around weight. Every rock has mass — you push it, lift it, stack it, or get shoved off a ledge by it. Terrain is a hand-painted image, and the colors are the physics: yellow slopes drag you left, gray sand slows you down, green means you can climb it.

  • Push / lift / stack physics with real mass
  • Color-coded painted terrain — ramps, dense sand, climbable walls
  • Roaming coconut enemies that notice you and shove back
  • Stages: Endless Desert · Sand Bank · Painted Isle · Bone Pit

Flying Dungeon

Shoot-'em-up · Browser · itch.io

A jam game that got out of hand. Pilot a fruit plane around a dungeon that orbits — turn left or right and the whole room spins with you. Three pilots, one machine gun, no brakes. Built standalone, written so it folds back into Saborosa later.

  • Rotating dungeon that turns as you bank
  • Three pilots: lemon, tomato, eggplant
  • Hand-drawn frame-by-frame animation throughout
  • Runs in the browser — no install

The studio

Saborosa Games is a tiny independent studio. We make games the slow way: pen on paper, scanned, animated frame by frame, then dropped into an engine we wrote ourselves.

No asset packs, no pixel-art filter, no middleware. If it moves on screen, someone here drew it moving. That's the whole pitch — and it's why our games look like nothing else on the shelf.
